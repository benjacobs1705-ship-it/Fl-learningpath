# Roadmap

## 1. Functional kick builder
TypeScript + Vite; oscillator, pitch envelope, amplitude envelope, transient, drive, playback, waveform, 24-bit WAV export, tests.

## 2. Advanced sound shaping
Editable multistage pitch curves, separated punch/body/tail, filtering, multiband distortion, oversampling, output limiting and A/B listening.

## 3. Creative FX
Automation lanes; transform a kick into a riser, screech or impact; save/load presets as JSON.

## 4. Learning mode
Dutch guided lessons, exercises, listening comparisons and local progress tracking.

## 5. Packaging research
Explore a desktop build and VST3 feasibility after the audio engine is stable.

## Acceptance
No fake controls; audio output must be reproducible and WAV export validated. Keep user audio and data local.
